How to play

  • Move — ← / → on the D-pad (hold to repeat) or swipe the board horizontally.
  • Rotate — ↻ at the top of the D-pad, or tap the board.
  • Soft drop — ↓ at the bottom of the D-pad, or swipe down.
  • Hard drop — the DROP button.
  • Pause — Ⅱ in the top corner.

FAQ

The D-pad is on the wrong side. Open the side panel (“?”) and switch “Hand: right / left”. The choice is remembered.

No haptics. Check the haptics toggle in the side panel and the system “Sounds & Haptics” settings.

The game resumed at the wrong point. Progress is auto-saved every two seconds and when the app goes to the background; it reopens paused.

The game is fully offline, with no account and no ads. Privacy Policy.
