Who we are

This website is operated by WoofWoof Tech MB, a company from Lithuania (company code 308157323, registered office Perkūnkiemio g. 19, LT-12120 Vilnius). WoofWoof Tech MB is the controller responsible for the personal data described in this policy. Contact: privacy@woofwoof.tech.

What this website doesn't do

The site sets no cookies of its own and stores nothing in your browser. It uses no analytics or behavioural tracking, no advertising, no tracking pixels and no social-media plugins or embedded videos, and it loads nothing from third-party servers. There are no accounts, sign-ups or forms, so you don't submit personal information through the website itself.

Visiting the site

When you open this page, the services that host and deliver this website receive the technical data that every web request contains: your IP address, the date and time, the page requested, your browser's user-agent string and, if your browser sends it, the referring page. This data is used only to deliver the site and to keep it secure, including in security logs. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in running a working, secure website (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). Logs are kept only as long as that purpose requires; an entry needed to investigate a specific security incident is kept until the incident is closed.

Emailing us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include — for example your name, your message and any attachments. We use it to reply and to deal with your request. If your message is about working with us or about a contract, the legal basis is taking the steps you asked for or performing that contract (Article 6(1)(b)); otherwise it is our legitimate interest in answering messages sent to us (Article 6(1)(f)). Writing to us is voluntary; we simply need an address to reply to.

How long we keep emails

We keep general correspondence only for as long as we need it to deal with your enquiry and any follow-up. If an email becomes part of a contract or our accounting records, we keep it for as long as Lithuanian law requires — for contracts and accounting documents this is generally 10 years (Article 6(1)(c)). We keep a message longer only if we need it to establish, exercise or defend a legal claim, and only until that matter is closed.

Who else handles the data

Our email and hosting providers process this data on our behalf as our service providers. We don't sell personal data or share it for advertising, and we disclose it to public authorities only where the law requires it. If a provider processes data outside the European Economic Area, the transfer is covered by an EU adequacy decision (such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework) or by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses; you can ask us for details.

Your rights

The GDPR gives you rights over your personal data, whether you are in the European Economic Area or elsewhere. You can ask us for a copy of it, ask us to correct or delete it, or ask us to restrict how we use it; where we process it to perform a contract with you, you can also ask for it in a portable format. Email privacy@woofwoof.tech — we reply within one month.

Your right to object

Where we rely on our legitimate interests (server logs, general emails, legal claims), you can object at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation. We will then stop, unless we have compelling legitimate grounds or need the data to establish, exercise or defend a legal claim. On request, we will explain how we balanced our interests against yours.

Complaints

You can complain to the Lithuanian State Data Protection Inspectorate (Valstybinė duomenų apsaugos inspekcija, vdai.lrv.lt) or to the data protection authority in the EEA country where you live or work (list of EEA authorities).

Also worth knowing

We don't profile visitors or make decisions about anyone based solely on automated processing. This website is meant for business contacts and is not directed at children. Each of our apps will have its own privacy policy, linked from its store listing; this policy covers only this website and email contact with us.

Changes

If we change how we use personal data in a way that matters to you, we will update this policy and the date above. If we are already in contact with you, we will also tell you directly.
